    How to request a ride

    Get started

    Enter your destination on Uber.com or in the Uber app. Choose a ride and confirm.

    Get matched with a driver

    A nearby driver accepts your request. You'll be notified when they're close.

    Meet your driver

    Verify your driver's name, vehicle details and your destination, then enter the vehicle.

    Enjoy the ride

    Your driver has turn-by-turn directions for the fastest route.

    Rate and tip

    Please leave feedback about your trip, and
    you’ll also be able to tip your driver.
